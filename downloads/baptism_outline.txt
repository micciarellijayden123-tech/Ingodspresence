
Baptism Outline

Purpose
This guide provides a Scripture-based framework for planning a baptism service. Baptism expresses a believer's union with Christ in his death, burial, and resurrection (Romans 6:3-4).

Preparation
- Meet with the candidate to review the gospel and confirm their faith in Jesus Christ (Romans 10:9-10).
- Invite the candidate to share a brief testimony of how they came to faith.
- Confirm logistics: clothing, towels, baptism location, witnesses, and any technical needs.
- Pray with the candidate and encourage them to invite family and friends.

Service structure
1. Welcome and opening remarks
   - Read Matthew 28:18-20.
   - Explain the meaning of baptism as a public declaration of following Jesus.
2. Testimony
   - Invite the candidate to share a brief personal testimony of faith.
3. Scripture reading
   - Recommended passages: Romans 6:3-4; Colossians 2:12; Acts 2:38.
4. Baptism ceremony
   - Conduct the baptism with a short statement, such as:
     "[Name], I baptize you in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit." (Matthew 28:19)
   - Use immersion or pouring according to your tradition.
5. Prayer and welcome
   - Pray for the baptized believer and celebrate their new identity in Christ.
   - Welcome them into the church community.

Follow-up
- Provide next steps: connect the new believer to a discipleship group, a Bible reading plan, and a mentor (Hebrews 10:24-25; 2 Timothy 2:2).
- Encourage consistent prayer, daily Scripture reading, and participation in church life.

Best practices
- Keep the theology clear and accessible.
- Focus on repentance, faith, and new life in Christ.
- Celebrate the baptism as a meaningful step in the believer's spiritual journey.
