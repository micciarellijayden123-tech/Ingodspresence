Baptism Outline

Purpose
A short outline to prepare and celebrate baptism with new believers.

Before the service
- Meet with the candidate to explain meaning and answer questions.
- Review basic testimony: how they trusted Christ.

Service outline
1) Welcome and explanation of baptism
2) Candidate testimony (brief)
3) Scripture reading (e.g., Matthew 28:18-20; Romans 6:3-4)
4) Baptism (immersion or pouring)
5) Prayer and blessing

After the service
- Provide simple next steps: discipleship group, Bible reading plan, community support.
