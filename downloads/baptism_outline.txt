
Baptism Outline

Purpose
This outline helps leaders prepare and celebrate baptism rooted in Scripture. Baptism is an outward sign of an inward faith (Romans 6:3-4; Matthew 28:19-20).

Before the service
- Meet with the candidate to explain the meaning of baptism and ensure they understand the gospel (Romans 10:9-10).
- Confirm a simple testimony: how they came to trust Christ and their desire to follow him.
- Practical checks: clothing, towel, witnesses, and the location for baptism.

Service outline
1) Welcome and brief explanation
	- Read: Matthew 28:18-20 and explain baptism as a public declaration of following Jesus.
2) Candidate testimony (2-3 minutes)
	- Invite the candidate to briefly tell how they trusted Jesus.
3) Scripture reading
	- Recommended: Romans 6:3-4, Colossians 2:12, Acts 2:38
4) Baptism act
	- Perform baptism (immersion or pouring) with a short phrase such as: "[Name], I baptize you in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit." (Matthew 28:19)
5) Prayer and welcome
	- Pray for the new believer and welcome them into the church family.

After the service
- Provide next steps: connect to a discipleship group, give a Bible reading plan, and assign a small-group mentor (Hebrews 10:24-25; 2 Timothy 2:2).

Practical tips
- Keep the theology simple and Bible-centred. Emphasize repentance, faith, and new life in Christ. Celebrate, pray, and follow up with discipleship.
