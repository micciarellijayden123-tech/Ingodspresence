Gospel Conversation Guide

Introduction
This short guide helps you start spiritual conversations, listen well, and share the gospel clearly.

1) Ask a warm question
- "How have you been? What gives your life meaning?"

2) Listen and follow up
- Reflect what they say and ask gentle follow-ups.

3) Share the good news briefly
- "God loves you. Jesus lived, died, and rose to bring us back to God. We can know him personally by turning from sin and trusting Jesus."

4) Invite
- "Would you like to talk more or read this short passage together?"

Pray and follow up
Ask if you can pray for them and schedule a follow-up conversation.
