
7-Day Discipleship Plan

Overview
This one-week discipleship plan provides a structured approach to help new believers grow in faith, prayer, Scripture engagement, and community.

Day 1: Salvation and New Identity
- Read: John 3:16; Romans 10:9-10
- Reflect: What does it mean to trust Jesus as Lord and Savior?
- Action: Encourage the participant to share a brief testimony of faith.
- Pray: Ask God to help them walk in their new identity.

Day 2: Prayer and Spiritual Dependence
- Read: Matthew 6:5-13
- Reflect: What does it mean to pray honestly before God?
- Practice: Use a simple prayer pattern of adoration, confession, thanksgiving, and supplication.
- Action: Pray together and commit to daily prayer.

Day 3: The Bible and Daily Reading
- Read: Psalm 119:105; 2 Timothy 3:16-17
- Reflect: How does Scripture guide life and faith?
- Practice: Read one short passage and ask: What does it say? What does it mean? How will I obey?
- Action: Read a passage daily and share one application.

Day 4: Baptism and New Life in Christ
- Read: Romans 6:3-4; Colossians 2:12
- Reflect: What does baptism symbolize in the life of a believer?
- Discuss: The connection between Christ’s death, resurrection, and our new life.
- Action: Plan next steps for baptism if appropriate.

Day 5: Community and Church Participation
- Read: Hebrews 10:24-25; Acts 2:42-47
- Reflect: Why is community essential for Christian growth?
- Discuss: Practical ways to belong to a church family.
- Action: Attend a service or join a small group.

Day 6: Serving and Using Spiritual Gifts
- Read: 1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:4-8
- Reflect: How can God use your gifts to bless others?
- Discuss: Identify one practical way to serve this week.
- Action: Serve someone through a simple act of kindness or service.

Day 7: Witnessing and Next Steps
- Read: Matthew 28:18-20; Acts 1:8
- Reflect: What does it mean to share the gospel with others?
- Practice: Apply the Gospel Conversation Guide in a real conversation.
- Action: Set a rhythm for ongoing discipleship, prayer, and Scripture reading.

Additional guidance
- Use the Gospel Conversation Guide and Baptism Outline provided in this resource folder.
- Help the believer connect with a local church and a mature Christian mentor for ongoing growth (2 Timothy 2:2).
