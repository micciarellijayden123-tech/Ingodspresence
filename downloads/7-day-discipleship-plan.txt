
7-Day Discipleship Plan

Day 1: Salvation & New Life
- Read: John 3:16; Romans 10:9-10
- Talk: What does it mean to trust Jesus? Encourage the person to tell their story.
- Pray: Ask God to help them follow Jesus.

Day 2: Prayer & Dependence on God
- Read: Matthew 6:5-13
- Practice: Teach a simple model for prayer (Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving, Supplication).
- Task: Pray together for 5 minutes each day.

Day 3: The Bible & How to Read It
- Read: Psalm 119:105; 2 Timothy 3:16-17
- Teach: Read one short passage, ask: What does it say? What does it mean? How will I obey?
- Task: Read one short passage daily and share one sentence of application.

Day 4: Baptism & New Identity
- Read: Romans 6:3-4; Colossians 2:12
- Talk: Explain baptism as identification with Christ's death and resurrection.
- Task: Discuss practical next steps toward baptism if ready.

Day 5: Church Community
- Read: Hebrews 10:24-25; Acts 2:42-47
- Talk: The importance of gathering, prayer, and fellowship.
- Task: Visit a Sunday service or a small group together.

Day 6: Serving & Gifts
- Read: 1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:4-8
- Talk: Everyone has gifts to serve others. Identify one way to serve this week.
- Task: Take one practical step to serve someone in the community.

Day 7: Sharing the Gospel & Next Steps
- Read: Matthew 28:18-20; Acts 1:8
- Practice: Use the Gospel Conversation Guide to share the gospel with one person.
- Plan: Set a simple ongoing discipleship rhythm (regular check-ins, Bible reading, prayer).
