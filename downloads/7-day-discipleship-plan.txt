7-Day Discipleship Plan

Day 1: Introduction & Salvation
- Read: John 3:16, Romans 10:9-10
- Discuss: What does it mean to trust Jesus?

Day 2: Prayer
- Read: Matthew 6:5-13
- Practice: Short guided prayer

Day 3: Bible Reading
- Read: Psalm 119:105; 2 Timothy 3:16-17
- Practice: How to read a passage and ask 3 questions

Day 4: Baptism Preparation
- Read: Romans 6:3-4
- Discuss: When and why to be baptized

Day 5: Community
- Read: Hebrews 10:24-25
- Discuss: Joining a small group

Day 6: Serving
- Read: 1 Peter 4:10
- Discuss: Using gifts to serve others

Day 7: Sharing the Gospel
- Read: Matthew 28:18-20
- Practice: Share Gospel conversation steps
