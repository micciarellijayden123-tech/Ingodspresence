The Great Commission

Matthew 28:19 — Go and make disciples of all nations.

This page outlines practical steps, teaching outlines, and baptism guidance to help individuals and churches obey Jesus' command.

Focus

Three core actions

Make disciples • Baptize • Teach

Practical steps for disciple-making

Start with prayer

Pray for boldness, opportunities, and open hearts to receive the Gospel.

Share the Gospel

Use simple, clear explanations of the Gospel and invite questions.

Follow up

Walk with new believers through study, encouragement, and community.

Baptism guide

Why baptism?

Baptism is an outward sign of inward faith and new life in Christ.

How to baptize

Simple script: declaration of faith, prayer, immersion or sprinkling according to local practice.

Next steps

Connect new believers with a small group and follow-up materials.
